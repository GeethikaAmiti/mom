A small letter from my heart

Mumma,

Some of the happiest and safest parts of my life have always had you in them.

You love quietly, care endlessly, and give so much of yourself to everyone around you. You do it so naturally that sometimes people forget how extraordinary you truly are.

You make home feel softer. You make hard days feel survivable. You make love feel simple, steady, and pure.

Thank you for every meal, every prayer, every worry, every scolding, every sacrifice, every smile, and every little thing you do even when nobody notices.

No matter how old I grow, a part of me will always feel safest beside you.

Home was never a place.
It was always you.

I love you more than I can ever properly explain.
